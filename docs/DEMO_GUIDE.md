# 3–5 Minute Interview Demo

## 0:00–0:35 — Executive situation

Open `/workspace/overview`. Explain that every KPI is calculated from 2,592 PO lines and linked quality/delivery data. Point out identified opportunity, adverse PPV and the Management Attention queue.

**Takeaway:** ProcureIQ starts with a management decision, not a decorative dashboard.

## 0:35–1:05 — Spend driver

Open **Spend Intelligence**, filter Category to **Aluminium**, and show the changed KPIs, spend trend, Pareto curve and ABC table. Explain PPV:

```text
(actual unit price − standard price) × actual quantity
```

**Takeaway:** Aluminium inflation and supplier pricing—not demand alone—drive adverse value.

## 1:05–1:45 — Cheapest is not cheapest

Open **Suppliers**, choose **Arka Precision Metals**, and show the defect, OTD and operational-risk evidence. Then open **Supplier Comparison** for the Aluminium Bracket. Distinguish lowest quotation from recommended supplier and read the generated “Why this supplier?” statement.

**Takeaway:** freight, quality, inventory and delay can erase a low quotation.

## 1:45–2:30 — Should-cost

Open **Should-Cost** → **Aluminium Bracket**. Change raw-material rate from ₹314/kg to ₹330/kg or adjust Milling cycle time. Show that target cost, range and annual negotiation opportunity recalculate immediately. Point to gross material, scrap recovery, conversion, overhead and margin.

**Takeaway:** the negotiation position is operation-level and defensible.

## 2:30–3:25 — RFQ and optimization

Open **RFQ Analysis** for `RFQ-001`. Show lowest quotation, lowest evaluated cost and recommended offer. Then open **Sourcing Optimizer**, keep the 60% maximum share and run optimization. Explain demand balance, capacity, MOQ, risk/quality eligibility and the multi-supplier allocation.

**Takeaway:** a feasible dual-source mix can balance cost with resilience.

## 3:25–4:00 — Disruption

Open **Scenario Lab**, select **Supplier Capacity Loss**, and run it. Compare TCO and risk deltas and explain why the optimizer should be rerun with the new capacity.

**Takeaway:** sourcing decisions are conditional; ProcureIQ shows when the preferred strategy changes.

## Strong closing sentence

“ProcureIQ combines procurement analytics, cost engineering and operations research so that I can explain not only what happened, but which sourcing decision is economically and operationally defensible.”
